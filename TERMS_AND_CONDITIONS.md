## 2. Booking Confirmation

### 2.1 Booking Request

Submitting the booking form creates a **booking request**. It does not
automatically guarantee or confirm the reservation.

Each request is issued a booking reference beginning with `DL-BK`. Please quote
this reference in all correspondence about your stay.

### 2.2 Confirmation

A reservation is considered **confirmed only after**:

1. D'Lux Homes approves the booking; and
2. The required down payment has been received and verified.

Guests should not consider their reservation final until confirmation has been
sent by D'Lux Homes.

### 2.3 Booking Stages

A booking passes through the following stages. The guest is notified by email at
each one:

1. **Request received** — the booking has been submitted and is awaiting review.
2. **Approved or declined** — D'Lux Homes reviews the request and the payment.
3. **Confirmed** — the booking is approved and the down payment is verified. The
   dates are now held for the guest.
4. **Checked in** — the stay has begun.
5. **Checked out** — the stay has ended and the security deposit is processed.

### 2.4 Booking Information

The booking confirmation and checkout summary contain the specific details
applicable to the guest's reservation, including:

- Stay type;
- Date;
- Number of guests;
- Check-in time;
- Check-out time;
- Applicable charges;
- Payment information.

Guests are responsible for reviewing these details before completing their
booking.

### 2.5 Availability

Reservations are subject to availability.

Submitting a booking request does not guarantee that the requested date or time
will remain available until the reservation is approved and confirmed.

### 2.6 Expired Requests

A booking request that has not been approved, or whose down payment has not been
verified, by the time its check-in date passes is treated as **expired**. An
expired request does not entitle the guest to the unit. Where a down payment was
received for a request that expires without being confirmed, it is returned in
accordance with §7.7.

### 2.7 Rejection

D'Lux Homes may decline any booking request at its discretion, including where
the requested dates are no longer available, where guest details or
identification are incomplete or inconsistent, or where payment cannot be
verified. Refunds on rejection are governed by §7.7.

## 4. Guest Capacity

### 4.1 Standard Occupancy

The standard booking rate covers up to **2 guests**, subject to the applicable
rate displayed during checkout.

### 4.2 Additional Guests

Guests aged **7 and above** count toward the guest capacity and toward the rate.

Each counted guest beyond the standard occupancy of 2 is subject to an
additional guest charge. For Overnight stays this charge applies **per night**.
For Daycation and Nightcation stays, which are single sessions, it applies once.

The applicable additional guest charge is displayed during checkout before the
booking is submitted.

### 4.3 Children

Children **under 7 years of age** stay free of the additional guest charge and
are not counted toward the rate.

A maximum of **4** such children may be included in a booking.

### 4.4 Maximum Capacity

The maximum number of **counted guests** (aged 7 and above) that may be booked
online is **4**.

Requests exceeding the online capacity may be discussed directly with D'Lux
Homes through its official communication channels.

### 4.5 Accuracy of Guest Count

Guests must provide the correct number of people who will stay in the unit.

Bringing additional or unregistered guests without prior approval may result in
additional charges, security-deposit deductions, or termination of the stay.

## 6. Rates and Charges

### 6.1 Applicable Rate

The price shown in the booking summary at checkout is the applicable price for
the reservation.

Rates may change for future bookings without prior notice. Changes to future
published rates will not affect an already submitted or confirmed reservation.

All amounts are in **Philippine Pesos (₱)**.

### 6.2 Weekday, Weekend, and Holiday Rates

Rates differ between weekdays and weekends or Philippine holidays.

For ordinary stays, **each night is priced according to its own date**. A stay
that spans both weekdays and weekends will therefore combine both rates, and the
total shown at checkout reflects that combination.

### 6.3 Long-Stay Pricing

Where an Overnight booking reaches the qualifying number of nights set by D'Lux
Homes, the **entire stay** is repriced at a flat nightly rate instead of being
priced night by night, and a separate long-stay additional guest charge applies
in place of the charge described in §4.2. The two additional guest charges never
both apply to the same booking.

The qualifying night bands and their rates are set by D'Lux Homes and are
reflected in the quotation shown at checkout, which governs.

### 6.4 Other Charges

Additional charges may apply depending on the reservation or the stay,
including:

- Additional guests (§4.2);
- Late check-out (§10.2);
- Damage or loss (§14);
- Excessive cleaning (§12.1);
- Other charges expressly stated in these Terms or in the booking summary.

Charges known at the time of booking are displayed during checkout. Charges
arising from the stay itself are assessed after check-out in accordance with
§11.

### 6.5 Promotions

Promotional offers and voucher codes are subject to their stated conditions,
validity period, applicable stay types, and redemption limits.

A promotion that has been successfully applied to a submitted booking will be
honored for that booking according to its applicable conditions.

### 6.6 Senior Citizen and PWD Discounts

In accordance with Republic Act No. 9994 and Republic Act No. 10754, a
qualifying Senior Citizen or Person with Disability receives **20% off that
guest's own share of the accommodation charge**. The guest's share is the
accommodation total divided evenly among the counted guests. The discount is
**not** 20% of the whole booking total, and it does not apply to the additional
guest charge.

To claim the discount, the qualifying guest must be identified during checkout
and their date of birth provided.

A valid Senior Citizen or PWD ID must be presented at check-in. If the required
ID cannot be presented, the discount may be reversed and the difference may
become payable before the stay begins.

## 8. Cancellation and Date Changes

> **Please read this section carefully before paying your down payment.**
>
> D'Lux Homes does **not** cancel or refund confirmed bookings. What is offered
> instead is **one free date change**, and only if it is requested **at least 7
> days before check-in**. Once a booking is confirmed, this is the only way to
> move a stay.

### 8.1 Summary

| Situation | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Request withdrawn before D'Lux Homes approves it | Down payment returned in full (§8.3) |
| Request declined by D'Lux Homes, or expired | Down payment returned in full (§7.7) |
| Confirmed booking, guest cancels | **No refund.** Amounts paid are forfeited (§8.2) |
| Confirmed booking, date change requested **7+ days** before check-in | One free date change, subject to §8.5 |
| Confirmed booking, date change requested **less than 7 days** before check-in | At D'Lux Homes' discretion; may be refused (§8.9) |
| Guest does not arrive | Treated as a no-show. Amounts paid are forfeited (§8.11) |
| D'Lux Homes cancels a confirmed booking | Refunded under §21 |

### 8.2 No Cancellation of Confirmed Bookings

Confirmed bookings are **non-cancellable and non-refundable**.

This means that once a booking is confirmed, a guest who no longer wishes to
proceed with the stay is **not entitled to a refund** of the down payment, of
the balance, or of any other amount paid for the booking.

If a guest chooses to cancel a confirmed booking — including by using any
cancellation option available in their booking account — the dates are released
and made available to other guests, and **the amounts already paid are
forfeited**. Cancelling does not generate a refund, a credit, or a right to
reschedule.

The only exceptions are:

- where a refund is required by applicable law;
- where the down payment falls under §7.7 or §8.3; or
- where D'Lux Homes cancels the reservation under §21.

### 8.3 Withdrawing a Request Before Confirmation

Because the down payment is collected at checkout, before D'Lux Homes has
reviewed the request (§7.1), a guest may withdraw a booking request at any time
**before it has been approved and confirmed**.

A request withdrawn at that stage is treated in the same way as a declined or
expired request, and the down payment is **returned in full** in accordance with
§7.7.

Once the booking is confirmed, §8.2 applies and this option is no longer
available.

### 8.4 How to Request a Cancellation or Date Change

Requests must be sent to **homesdlux@gmail.com**, or through the D'Lux Homes
official Facebook page, and must include:

- The booking reference (beginning `DL-BK`);
- The name of the Main Guest;
- The original check-in date; and
- For a date change, the preferred new date and stay type.

**The date of the request is the date D'Lux Homes receives it**, not the date
the guest decided to make it. Requests sent to any other address or channel, or
left as a message that does not reach D'Lux Homes, do not count as received.

### 8.5 One-Time Date Change

The guest may request **one date change per booking**, free of charge, if **all**
of the following conditions are met:

- The request is received at least **7 days before the original check-in date**;
- The new check-in date falls **within 1 month of the original check-in date**;
  and
- The unit is available for the requested date and stay type.

The entitlement is **one change per booking**. Once a date change has been
confirmed, the booking has used its free change, and any further request falls
under §8.9 — including a request to move back to the original date.

### 8.6 Worked Examples

For a booking with an original check-in date of **Friday, 10 October**:

| Request | Result |
|---|---|
| Received 1 October, for a new check-in on 24 October | **Within both limits.** 9 days' notice, and the new date is inside 1 month. Free change, subject to availability |
| Received 3 October, for a new check-in on 9 November | **Within both limits.** Exactly 7 days' notice, and the new date is inside 1 month |
| Received 7 October, for a new check-in on 24 October | **Outside the notice period.** Only 3 days' notice. Discretionary under §8.9 |
| Received 1 October, for a new check-in on 20 November | **Outside the 1-month window.** Discretionary under §8.9 |
| Received 1 October, but the requested date is already booked | Not available. The guest may propose another date within the same limits |

Both conditions must be satisfied. Meeting one does not create an entitlement.

### 8.7 What Carries Over to the New Dates

Where a date change is confirmed:

- The down payment already made is applied to the rescheduled booking;
- The booking keeps its original booking reference; and
- The guest list, stay type, and guest count carry over unless a change to them
  is requested and approved at the same time.

A change to the **stay type** (for example from Daycation to Overnight), or to
the **number of nights**, is not a date change. It is treated as a new booking
and is subject to availability and to the rates in effect at the time of the
request.

### 8.8 Rate Differences on a Date Change

Rates vary by date (§6.2). Where the new dates carry a **higher** rate than the
original booking — for example, moving a weekday stay onto a weekend or a
holiday — the difference is payable before the rescheduled booking is confirmed.

Where the new dates carry a **lower** rate, the difference is **not** refunded
and is not credited against a future booking.

### 8.9 Late and Additional Requests

A date-change request that is received less than 7 days before check-in, that
falls outside the 1-month window, or that is the second or later request on the
same booking, may be considered **at the sole discretion of D'Lux Homes**.

Such a request may be refused, or may be accommodated on conditions, including
payment of any difference in rate. A previous accommodation does not create an
entitlement to another.

### 8.10 Approval and Confirmation

No cancellation or date change takes effect until D'Lux Homes has confirmed it
in writing.

A request that has been sent, acknowledged, or discussed is **not** an approved
change. Guests must not travel on, or rely on, new dates until they have
received written confirmation, and the original booking remains in force until
that confirmation is issued.

### 8.11 No-Show and Late Arrival

If the guest does not arrive during the check-in period stated in the confirmed
booking, and has not notified D'Lux Homes, the reservation is treated as a
**no-show**.

A no-show ends the booking. Amounts already paid are forfeited, subject to
applicable law, and the unit may be released.

Arriving late within the stay does not extend the stay or the check-out time,
and does not reduce the amount payable.

### 8.12 Circumstances Beyond the Guest's Control

Illness, travel disruption, weather, work commitments, and similar events
affecting the guest do not, by themselves, create a right to a refund or to a
free date change outside the limits in §8.5.

Guests facing such circumstances are encouraged to contact D'Lux Homes as early
as possible. Requests will be considered under §8.9, but accommodation is at
D'Lux Homes' discretion.

Guests who want protection against these events should consider arranging their
own travel insurance.
